Paste a date heading (for example “Mon 2nd Nov AM” or “02/11/2026 evening”) then one reading per line, such as “162 89” or “162/89/60” (pulse optional). AM/PM or morning/evening sets the session; surgery, GP or afternoon becomes Surgery / other. With no heading, readings go in as one session using the date and session type chosen in the form above.

Finalise builds a report from every saved session. You can keep recording afterwards and finalise again. To correct a date or reading, tap Edit on that session in History, then finalise again.

How to use this app

  1. Your details (optional). On the Record tab, enter your name and date of birth. They are only used in the report.
  2. Take your readings. Enter each one in a row: systolic, diastolic, and pulse if you want it. Five readings is usual. Tap “+ Add reading” if you take more.
  3. Save the session. Choose the date and the session (Morning, Evening or Surgery / other), then tap Save session. The median of the readings is worked out for you.
  4. Repeat for as many days as you like. The History tab lists every session, newest first.
  5. Finalise. On the Report tab, tap Finalise report. You can then Share it, Copy it for Notes, Save it as a PDF or Email it.
  6. Made a mistake? Tap Edit on a session, in History or on a row of the report, change the date or any reading, then tap Update session. The report refreshes. Delete is in History.

Good to know

  • Median is the middle reading when they are put in order. If there is an even number, it is the average of the middle two.
  • Report averages are the averages of the session medians. A median of 135/85 or above is shown in red.
  • Finalising does not delete or lock anything. Keep recording and finalise again whenever you like.
  • Took an evening reading after midnight? Choose the date of the evening it belongs to.
  • Importing from notes: on the Record tab, paste a heading such as “Mon 2nd Nov AM”, then one reading per line, such as “162 89”, and tap Import sessions.

Your data

Everything is stored only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you share or email a report. Clearing your browser’s site data will erase your readings, so take a backup now and then.

To restore a backup, paste it into the Import box on the Record tab and tap Import sessions.

This is a personal record, not a medical device, and it does not give medical advice. Talk to your GP or nurse about your readings.
